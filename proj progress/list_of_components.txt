List of Components for Automatic Irrigation Project
===================================================

1. Arduino Board & Accessories:
   - 1 x Arduino Uno R3
   - 1 x USB Cable (Type-A to Type-B)

2. Sensors & Modules:
   - 1 x Soil Moisture Sensor Module
   - 1 x 5V 1-Channel Relay Module

3. Actuators & Power Supply:
   - 1 x 5V Mini Submersible Water Pump
   - 1 x 5V DC Power Adapter (Power Supply)

4. Wiring & Tubing:
   - Male-to-Female Jumper Wires
   - Male-to-Male Jumper Wires
   - Flexible Silicone Hose / Tubing (for water pump)
